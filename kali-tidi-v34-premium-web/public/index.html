KALITIRI RULESET

Kali Teedi / Kali Tiri / 3 of Spades

8-player mode follows the supplied KaliTiri rules page. Three-, five- and seven-player tables are custom adaptations that remove only zero-point ranks so cards divide evenly without removing scoring points.

v22 fair-play & control

Legal move highlightOnly cards allowed by the follow-suit rule are highlighted on your turn.
Fast card playTap any highlighted legal card to play it immediately. No extra confirmation button is required.
Secure shuffleThe server uses a cryptographically secure Fisher–Yates shuffle and checks the deck before every deal.
Audit IDEvery round receives a unique audit ID and score/deck integrity status for dispute review.

PowerHouse & trick play

  1. The bid winner chooses one suit as the PowerHouse (Hukum) and explicitly chooses the exact card(s) that call the hidden partner(s).
  2. The bid winner leads the first trick; after that, the last trick winner leads.
  3. Everyone must follow the suit led when they can.
  4. If you cannot follow suit, a PowerHouse card can win the trick. A non-PowerHouse off-suit discard cannot win.
  5. If no PowerHouse is played, the highest card of the led suit wins. Card order is A, K, Q, J, 10, 9 … 2.
  6. 3♠ is a 30-point scoring card; it does not automatically outrank the lead suit or PowerHouse in this ruleset.
  7. With duplicate cards, when the same winning card appears again, the later/second identical copy wins.

Round scoring

Bidding team succeedsBidder + called partner(s) must score at least the bid. Example: bid 350 → 350 or more wins the contract.
Bidding team failsIf bidder + partner(s) finish below the bid, they lose the round and the opposite/defense team wins.
Opposing teamEach opposing player receives the points the opposing team captured, with no bid threshold.

How to play Kaali Ni Tidi

1BidBid for the points you believe your hidden team can capture.
2Choose HukumThe winning bidder chooses the PowerHouse suit and hidden partner cards.
3Follow suitPlay the led suit when possible. Hukum can win when you cannot follow.
4Reveal partnersA hidden partner is revealed when a called physical card is played.
5Beat the bidThe bidder team must finish with points equal to or greater than the winning bid.
