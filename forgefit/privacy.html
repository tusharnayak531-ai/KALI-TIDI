ForgeFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy describes ForgeFit V1.0 for iPhone, a local-first fitness tracking app.

Information you enter

ForgeFit may store workout details, meals, calorie and macronutrient totals, hydration logs, bodyweight records, and display name. These records are saved locally on your device; ForgeFit V1.0 has no account system or remote database. We do not receive or sell this information.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The native iPhone app does not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking identifiers.

Exporting your records

You can explicitly export a JSON backup using the iPhone Share Sheet or import one from Files. Any subsequent sharing with third-party services is your choice and governed by those services' policies.

Data deletion

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete all local data. Uninstalling the app also ordinarily deletes its local data, subject to any device backups that you control.

Children and medical information

ForgeFit is a general fitness log, not a medical service. Nutrition values are estimates and should not be used as medical guidance.

Contact

For privacy questions or help, use our support page.
